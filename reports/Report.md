## Introduction
This report shows the results of our PyTorch Lightning model. We trained it to see if a text was written by a human or generated by an AI using data like word count and readability. Below are the graphs that show how the model worked in training. There was not a train-test split, so results may be extremely optimistic

## 2. Confusion Matrix
<img src="figures/ConfusionMatrix.png" width="350">

**Caption:** This image shows the correct and incorrect predictions made by the model during the evaluation.
**Interpretation:** The model did a very good job here. The main diagonal is full of examples. This means the model completly undertanding the nature of the data and how to separate each group.
